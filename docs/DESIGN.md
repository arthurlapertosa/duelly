# Design

The product should feel like a simple 1:1 challenge platform, not a crypto application.

## Voice

- Direct.
- Friendly.
- Clear.
- Trust-building.
- Free of Web3 jargon in the primary flow.

## Initial components

- Template card.
- Create challenge screen.
- Invite screen.
- Confirmation screen.
- Bet status.
- Result and payout.

## Suggested copy

- “Choose your side.”
- “Invite your opponent.”
- “Your bet is confirmed.”
- “Waiting for the result.”
- “Result confirmed automatically.”
- “Amount received.”

## Evidence for design/frontend PRs

- Screenshots or short recordings when UI changes.
- Empty, loading, error, and success states documented.
- Basic accessibility verified.

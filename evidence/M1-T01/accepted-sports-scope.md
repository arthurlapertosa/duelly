# Accepted Sports Scope

M1 is sports-first. Collectibles are out of scope.

Accepted coverage:

- Football/Soccer: FIFA World Cup, FIFA Club World Cup or approved world-level competitions, Brasileirão, Copa Libertadores.
- Tennis: ATP 250, ATP 500, ATP Masters 1000, ATP Finals, and Grand Slams, including individual match winners and binary tournament winners.
- UFC: main events only.
- Formula 1: race winners and sprint race winners.

The backend implementation uses Node.js, TypeScript, Fastify, PostgreSQL, and TypeORM.
